The History of New York City

New York City was founded in 1624 as a trading post by colonists of the Dutch Republic.

Did you know? NYC was originally called New Amsterdam!

The Culture of NYC

New York City is known for its diversity, arts, music, and vibrant lifestyle.

Travel Tips

Top Tips for Tourists

  • Use the subway — it's the fastest way to get around.
  • Wear comfortable shoes — NYC requires lots of walking.
  • Visit museums on free-admission days.
